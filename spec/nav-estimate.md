# NAV Estimation Engine & Display

- **Current vs. Estimated NAV:** Display the latest official NAV (with date, e.g., NAV 11/20) alongside the projected next-business-day NAV (e.g., 11/23 NAV estimate) in Japanese Yen (円).
- **Percentage Change & Weather Indicator:** Calculate and display the projected percentage delta (e.g., -4.09%, -4.30%). Pair the numerical delta with an intuitive weather icon ("SunnyDay" motif—e.g., sun for gains, umbrella/rain for drops) to convey market sentiment at a glance.
- **Color-Coded Visuals:** Highlight positive projections in green/blue and negative projections in red for immediate scannability.
